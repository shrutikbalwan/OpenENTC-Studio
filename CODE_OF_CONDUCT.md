# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the members, contributors and maintainers of OpenENTC Studio, pledge to make taking part in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. This holds regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Many people here are students. We will act in ways that make an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy learning community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners and people writing in a second language
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, aimed at the work and not the person
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Submitting someone else's coursework or graded assignment as a contribution, or using the project to help academic dishonesty
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or classroom setting

## Responsibilities

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They will take fair corrective action in response
to behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of
Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and
any other channel run by the project. It also applies when someone officially represents the
project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainers listed in [docs/maintainers.md](docs/maintainers.md).

- **Placeholder:** a dedicated private conduct contact has not been set up yet (see
  [docs/security-contact-setup.md](docs/security-contact-setup.md)). Until it exists, contact the
  repository owner (@shrutikbalwan) through GitHub.
- If you need a private channel, open an issue that **only** asks for private contact. Include no
  names or details.
- All reports are reviewed and handled promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and
  security of the person reporting.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other unprofessional behaviour, the consequence is a
   private written warning explaining the problem. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or repeated actions, the consequence is a warning with
   consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a period of time.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour, the
   consequence is a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression
   toward or disparagement of groups of people, the consequence is a permanent ban.
